# G-4.23A Newport Street Grammar + Ground Cohesion Repair

TECHNICAL PASS DOES NOT EQUAL DESIGN PASS.

## Goal

Repair the Newport composition after G-4.22A so the town reads less like a
validated blockout and more like a coherent harbor city: a waterfront avenue,
roads uphill into town, a back street, grounded building lots, and unified
street/ground material language.

## Repairs Made

- Narrowed the harborfront road surface so it reads as a real avenue parallel
  to harbor work instead of a broad gray presentation band.
- Drew uphill connector roads before the avenue/back-street surfaces so west,
  central, east, and market routes remain visible from harbor to town.
- Tightened the inland/back-street surface behind the waterfront road and
  added yard/civic ground washes around it.
- Expanded surrounding city fabric into ordered upper and side frontage lines
  instead of isolated building drops.
- Added enclosed residential, civic, market, and service lot cells so major
  gaps read as yards, work courts, civic lawns, and market courts instead of
  raw green space.
- Re-seated clean-review dressing as functional support for streets, lots,
  wharf work, and thresholds; oversized cargo and old proof clutter remain
  gated away.
- Reintroduced the G-4.20A provenance-tracked atelier terrain, cobble/path,
  shoreline, and building-grounding placements in clean review.
- Moved the starter harbor-town camera closer to street/building scale without
  changing player sprite scale.
- Added G-4.23A screenshot automation while preserving the G-4.22A wrapper.
