# Newport Street Grammar Checklist

## Required Street Structure

- [ ] Waterfront avenue parallel to the harbor.
- [ ] At least two roads running uphill from the harborfront avenue.
- [ ] Back street behind the first avenue.
- [ ] Service alley route connecting town and wharf.
- [ ] Clear transition from dock boards to street to yards/grass.
- [ ] Roads have coherent width, material, and edge language.

## Lot And Building Placement

- [ ] Buildings define streets rather than floating beside paths.
- [ ] Buildings sit on coherent lots, yards, docks, or civic squares.
- [ ] Door/frontage positions meet the street.
- [ ] Fences/walls/hedges/stoops explain private and public boundaries.
- [ ] Negative space reads as street, yard, square, alley, dock apron, or garden.

## Artifact Failures

- [ ] Mismatched road layers are visible.
- [ ] Clipped transparent road/grass rectangles remain.
- [ ] Old proof overlays appear in clean review mode.
- [ ] Broad empty editor-like rectangles remain.
- [ ] Ground material changes do not match district logic.
