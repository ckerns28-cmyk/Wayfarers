# Newport Gameplay Space Checklist

## Player Movement

- [ ] Player can walk the harbor loop.
- [ ] Player can walk the market loop.
- [ ] Player can walk the civic/residential loop.
- [ ] Player can reach alleys, wharf, civic space, shops, and tavern.
- [ ] Routes are readable without debug overlays.

## NPC Plausibility

- [ ] Dock workers can move between docks, cargo, warehouses, and tavern/market.
- [ ] Vendors can plausibly stand at stalls without blocking primary movement.
- [ ] Residents can reach homes from back streets.
- [ ] Civic NPCs can reach public buildings and notice boards.
- [ ] Service alleys support work movement.

## Exploration Rhythm

- [ ] Major anchor appears every few seconds.
- [ ] Optional side routes exist without confusing the main route.
- [ ] Dead ends have a reason or an interaction.
- [ ] There is enough structure to support 90+ seconds of exploration in principle.
