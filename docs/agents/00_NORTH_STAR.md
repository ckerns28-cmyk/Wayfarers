# North Star / Creative Director Doctrine

Wayfarer is a handcrafted fantasy/colonial harbor RPG about believable exploration, visual wonder, social spaces, commerce, civic life, danger, and player curiosity.

Newport is the first major proof that the game can support a living starter harbor city. It is not a prop board, test grid, screenshot collage, or generic village blockout.

## World-Class Means

- A place with districts, routes, thresholds, landmarks, civic purpose, work life, class texture, and story pressure.
- Visual assets that feel authored into the world rather than pasted onto a debug plane.
- Player movement that exposes meaningful decisions every few seconds.
- NPC movement that could plausibly use streets, doors, docks, yards, alleys, market stalls, and civic spaces.
- Technical stability that supports the experience without becoming the only acceptance bar.

## Validators Are Required But Not Sufficient

A pass can satisfy import checks, vertical-slice validation, provenance validation, screenshot capture, and `git diff --check`, yet still fail as game design, art direction, world design, UX, or production scope.

The council must therefore separate:

- Technical validation
- Design acceptance
- Art-direction acceptance
- World/narrative acceptance
- UX/player acceptance
- Release recommendation

## Newport 8.5+/10 Bar

Newport approaches 8.5+/10 only when it reads as a coherent colonial harbor city with:

- finalized city structure before decoration: district bands, road hierarchy,
  avenue widths, wharf dimensions, block depth, lot spacing, and walkable
  camera composition,
- MMORPG-scale primary avenue and wharf widths that can plausibly fit roughly
  10-15 characters shoulder to shoulder,
- a clear waterfront avenue parallel to harbor work,
- roads running uphill into town,
- a back street behind the first road,
- civic, market, tavern, harbor, and residential districts,
- buildings grounded on coherent streets/lots/yards,
- unified street and ground materials,
- purposeful props and readable interaction anchors,
- player/NPC walkability that supports a village simulation,
- screenshot proof that visual composition and world logic agree.

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not hide layout failures with prop scatter.
- Do not hide ground/street failures with clutter.
- Do not let approved artwork compensate for an unresolved town plan.
- Do not promote temporary or uncertain art without provenance gates.
- Do not call technical pass equal to design pass.
- Never impersonate Chris or claim Chris approved a result.
