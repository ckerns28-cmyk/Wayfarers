# G-4.18C Green-Origin Visual Retrospective

## Result

G-4.18B was accepted as provenance/pipeline progress, not as normal-review
visual progress.

G-4.18C preserves the green-origin factory, manifests, contact sheets, reports,
and validators, but quarantines the failed proof art from the default
player-facing review build. The proof remains available only in Green-Origin
Lab mode (`F6` or `--show-green-origin-lab`).

## What G-4.18B Proved

- A separate `art_pipeline/newport_green_origin/` namespace can hold
  source-safe production experiments.
- Deterministic Python/Pillow generation can produce reproducible project-owned
  bitmaps from authored parameters.
- The manifest can prove that no yellow Newport building sprite, marketplace
  pack, ripped sheet, web image, or mystery PNG was opened, sampled, cropped,
  traced, or copied.
- Contact sheets and before/after comparisons can support future provenance
  review.

## Why The Visual Output Failed

The G-4.18B proof did not improve the player-facing screenshot. In context, the
pieces read as small pasted stickers rather than painterly harbor materials.

- `green_rope_coil_small` was too tiny and too isolated to read as useful dock
  dressing.
- `green_pier_post_pair` read as a detached sticker cluster rather than part of
  the wharf structure.
- `green_dock_plank_patch` and `green_dock_plank_strip` were off-scale beside
  the existing dock grammar and lacked enough authored integration.
- `green_dock_edge_shadow` and `green_plank_contact_shadow` were useful as
  tooling studies but too dark/noisy to carry a hero-quality surface.
- The combined proof cluttered the dock edge without making the Newport harbor
  feel more authored, coherent, or commercially ready.

## Tooling Lesson

Pillow/procedural generation is useful for provenance-safe tooling, contact
sheets, deterministic experiments, palette studies, collision masks, debug
atlases, and low-risk surface tests. It is not sufficient by itself for
hero-quality painterly sprite art at the current Wayfarer bar.

Future green-origin production should keep the deterministic recordkeeping but
move toward a stronger art method: hand-authored pixel/paintover work,
AI-assisted generation with complete source records and human cleanup,
Blender/procedural blockout plus paintover, or a hybrid style-study path that
does not copy yellow pixels into green final art.

## Required Next Attempt

G-4.18D should run an art production method bakeoff before any replacement path
is promoted. A candidate method must be judged on visual quality, repeatability,
editability, source clarity, Newport 1700s fantasy cohesion, and screenshot
improvement. Green-origin is necessary but not sufficient.
